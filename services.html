Service 01

Mold Testing: Scientific Air & Surface Analysis

When mold is suspected in your home, uncertainty can lead to anxiety regarding your family's health and the stability of your property. Our testing provides definitive scientific answers through expert air and surface evaluations.

Given Burlington County's proximity to the Delaware River and New Jersey's humid seasonal swings, moisture conditions frequently create optimal environments for concealed mold proliferation. Our testing protocol includes:

✔
Scientific Air Sampling: Measures spore counts per cubic meter inside your living areas compared to outdoor baseline controls.
✔
Surface Swab & Tape Lift Samples: Identifies whether specific surface discoloration is active fungal growth or benign cosmetic staining.
✔
Third-Party Certified Lab Reports: Independent analysis eliminates conflict of interest and provides clear documentation for realtors and insurance.

Service 02

Mold Inspection: Uncovering Hidden Moisture Issues

A mold inspection gives you a complete picture of your home's indoor environment, uncovering hidden dampness pockets, plumbing leaks, and crawlspace moisture before they escalate into catastrophic repairs.

Burlington and Mercer County feature a mix of historic construction, older plumbing systems, and modern slab foundations. We conduct a room-by-room assessment including basements, attics, crawl spaces, and HVAC closets using non-invasive diagnostic tools:

✔
Infrared Thermal Imaging: Maps cold moisture spots inside wall cavities and behind tile without punching unnecessary holes.
✔
Electronic Moisture Meters & Hygrometers: Measures structural wood saturation and indoor relative humidity levels.
✔
Actionable Remediation Roadmap: Documented photos and clear explanations outlining priorities, budget, and realistic next steps.

Mold Removal: Safe Containment & TAVS Vapor Remediation

Discovering mold in your property can feel overwhelming, but professional remediation restores safety, comfort, and peace of mind without aggressive demolition or excessive expense.

✔
Safe Containment & HEPA Air Scrubbers: High-efficiency particulate air filtration captures microscopic fungal particles during remediation.
✔
TAVS Vapor Fogging: Gaseous vapor penetrates into porous building substrates, wall cavities, and framing where hand-wiping cannot reach.
✔
Protective Antimicrobial Sealer: Creates an invisible shield that locks out future mold growth on wood and masonry for years.

Smoke Odor Removal: Deep-Penetrating Chlorine Dioxide

Are you dealing with persistent cigarette smells, tenant smoke damage, or stubborn musty odors? Surface masking with scented candles or retail sprays only temporarily hides odors. We eliminate them at the molecular level.

We utilize pure Chlorine Dioxide (ClO2) vapor technology. Chlorine dioxide is a powerful, non-residual oxidizer that bonds with smoke residue (tar and nicotine molecules) embedded deep inside drywall, subflooring, and ceiling plaster, neutralizing them permanently:

✔
Surface Wall Preparation: Direct wipe-down of walls and ceiling trim to remove surface tar layers.
✔
Deep Chlorine Dioxide Vapor Treatment: Penetrates textiles, framing, and insulation without leaving toxic residues.
✔
24-Hour Safety Protocol: Property owners vacate for 24 hours while the reaction takes place, returning to completely fresh, neutral air.

Air Duct Cleaning: RamAir System & SaniJet Botanical Disinfection

When it comes to indoor air quality, your HVAC ductwork plays a critical role. Over time, dust, allergens, pet dander, and mold spores accumulate in your ducts, constantly recirculating into your living spaces.

We use the revolutionary RamAir System, which combines mechanical agitation with powerful negative air vacuuming to pull contaminants out without spreading dust into your home. Furthermore, we pair this with the SaniJet Disinfecting System:

✔
Removes Up to 60% of Dust: Clearing duct buildup dramatically cuts dust settling on your furniture and floors.
✔
SaniJet Botanical Disinfection: Disperses an EPA-registered botanical disinfectant that kills 99.99% of EPA-tested microbes inside duct walls.
✔
Boosts HVAC Efficiency: Removing dust choke points improves airflow, reducing strain on blower motors and lowering energy bills.
