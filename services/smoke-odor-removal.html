Why Surface Masking Fails & How Chlorine Dioxide Works

Are you a homeowner, landlord, or realtor dealing with foul odors from former tobacco smokers or pet saturation? Tobacco smoke contains over 4,000 chemical compounds including tar, nicotine, and heavy oils. These microscopic particles do not stay in the air; they penetrate several millimeters into drywall plaster, wood subfloors, and insulation.

Simply painting over tobacco-stained walls with conventional latex paint will not solve the issue — the smell will bleed right through within weeks when humidity rises. Mold Recovery Plus follows an extremely thorough, guaranteed odor eradication process designed to eliminate foul smells permanently:

💨

Pure Chlorine Dioxide (ClO2) Gas Oxidation

Chlorine Dioxide is not a masking perfume or ozone generator. It is a powerful, EPA-proven selective oxidizer. Because it is a true gas, its molecules are small enough to follow the exact same path that smoke took, penetrating deep into drywall, cabinets, and subflooring to permanently break chemical bonds.

Our 3-Step Guaranteed Process

Step 01 — Written Assessment & Scope

We inspect the property, identify the primary odor reservoirs (carpets, HVAC ducts, unsealed walls), and present you with a clear written plan.

Step 02 — Comprehensive Surface Wipe-Down

Our technicians physically wipe down walls, baseboards, doors, and trim with specialized degreasing agents to remove surface nicotine oils and tar films before gas application.

Step 03 — Chlorine Dioxide Vapor Treatment (24-Hour Protocol)

We deploy our Chlorine Dioxide gas generators to flood the building envelope. You will need to vacate the property for 24 hours while the gas decontaminates the space. After 24 hours, the gas safely breaks down into harmless salt and water, leaving your home smelling completely neutral and fresh.
