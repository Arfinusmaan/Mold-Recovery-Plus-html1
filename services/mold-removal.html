Scientific Mitigation Without Destructive Demolition

Discovering mold in your home can feel overwhelming, but professional remediation restores safety, comfort, and peace of mind. Mold Removal in Burlington, NJ focuses on safe containment and remediation to minimize disruption inside your home, using industry-standard protocols that protect unaffected areas while technicians work.

Burlington's older housing stock, combined with seasonal humidity and closeness to waterways, means many properties face persistent moisture challenges that require more than cosmetic bleach or surface wipes. Bleach is water-based and merely bleaches the surface while feeding moisture to sub-surface fungal roots. Our remediation attacks mold at the cellular structure.

🛡️

Exclusive TAVS System (Technologically Advanced Vapor System)

Visible fungal colonies are first eradicated with our specialized surface treatment. Following stain removal, the entire enclosed envelope is treated with our proprietary TAVS vapor fogging, penetrating deep into framing pores and treating the air to eliminate airborne spores.

Our 5-Stage Remediation Protocol

1. Negative Pressure Containment & HEPA Filtration

We erect 6-mil poly containment barriers with zipper entries and run commercial negative-air HEPA scrubbers. This guarantees that disturbed spores during cleaning never drift into your living or sleeping areas.

2. Source Moisture Rectification

Mold cannot grow without water. We identify and assist in resolving the underlying moisture driver — whether that requires sump pump calibration, plumbing repair, or crawl space vapor barrier placement.

3. Active Surface Remediation & Stain Removal

Wood framing, joists, and cinder blocks are treated with our eco-friendly antimicrobial cleaners. Fungal stains are thoroughly broken down until the wood or masonry returns to clean, bright appearance.

4. TAVS Vapor Fogging

Our Technologically Advanced Vapor System fills the volume of the space, neutralizing airborne spores and treating hard-to-reach pockets inside floor cavities and truss connections.

5. Multi-Year Protective Barrier Sealer

We apply an antimicrobial polymer coating to the treated framing. This sealer bonds with the wood grain, preventing future mold spore germination even during periods of high summer humidity.
