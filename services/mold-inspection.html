Complete Home Evaluation by an IICRC Certified Specialist

A mold inspection gives you a complete picture of your home's indoor environment, revealing hidden moisture pockets and mold colonies that routine cleaning can't address. Mold Inspection in Burlington, NJ involves a thorough home evaluation including basements, attics, crawlspaces, and living areas using advanced tools to detect hidden dampness that escapes the naked eye.

Burlington's mix of historic colonial homes and modern suburban construction means each property presents unique vulnerabilities—from aging stone crawlspaces and unventilated attics to improperly flashed windows and condensation around air handlers.

🔍

Advanced Diagnostic Equipment

We deploy infrared thermal imaging cameras to reveal temperature differentials caused by hidden moisture behind walls, along with digital pinless wood moisture meters and calibrated hygrometers.

What Happens During a Professional Mold Inspection?

1. Room-by-Room Visual Assessment

We examine every accessible zone of concern: water staining, peeling paint, warped baseboards, efflorescence on foundation cinder blocks, and condensation rings around supply vents.

2. Thermal Imaging & Moisture Mapping

Infrared cameras scan ceilings, bathroom subflooring, and exterior-facing walls. Where thermal patterns indicate coolness, moisture meters measure structural wood saturation against baseline standards.

3. Attic & Crawl Space In-Depth Inspection

Attics and crawlspaces are the primary failure points in New Jersey homes. We inspect attic roof sheathing, bathroom vent exhausts that accidentally discharge into the attic, crawlspace dirt barriers, and floor joists.

4. Documented Photos & Action Plan

You receive labeled photographs with plain-English notes detailing where issues exist, why they formed, and a step-by-step recommendation that fits your property and budget.

Common Warning Signs That Justify an Inspection:

  • • Persistent Musty Odors: Smells that intensify during humid summer weather or when the HVAC runs.
  • • Recent Plumbing Leaks: Water heater overflows, leaking toilet flanges, or pipe bursts inside wall cavities.
  • • Visible Staining on Cinder Blocks: Dark streaks, white chalky powder, or discoloration on basement perimeter walls.
  • • Buying a Home in Central/South Jersey: Protecting your investment before closing with an independent inspection.
