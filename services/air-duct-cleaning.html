The Power of the RamAir Dual-Action Method

When it comes to indoor air quality, your home or office's air ducts play a critical role. Over time, dust, allergens, pet dander, mold spores, and construction debris accumulate inside your ductwork, affecting the air you breathe and forcing your HVAC blower motor to work harder.

Our professional air duct cleaning services use the innovative RamAir System to thoroughly and efficiently clean your ducts without creating a mess. Unlike traditional rotary brush methods that can tear flexible duct liners or knock dust loose into your rooms, RamAir uses advanced technology that combines the mechanical agitation cleaning method with powerful negative air vacuuming to trap and remove particles safely.

🌿

SaniJet System with EPA-Approved Botanical Disinfectant

For a deeper clean, we pair RamAir with our SaniJet disinfecting system. SaniJet atomizes an EPA-registered botanical disinfectant that kills 99.99% of EPA-tested microbes on duct walls, neutralizing mold spores and bacteria without toxic synthetic fumes.

How Our Duct Cleaning Process Works

1. Inspection & Vent Preparation

We assess the duct layout, remove supply and return grilles, and connect our clear RamAir vacuum box directly over each register opening.

2. High-Velocity Agitation & Extraction

Our compressed air blast wand is inserted through the duct trunk. Oscillating 360-degree air bursts blast dust off duct walls, while high-powered vacuum suction pulls the debris into filtered collectors.

3. SaniJet Botanical Disinfection

We feed the SaniJet system through each trunk, misting an EPA-approved thyme-based botanical disinfectant that kills 99.99% of bacteria and fungi without chemicals that trigger asthma.
