Scientific Air & Surface Sampling for Clear Answers

When mold is suspected in your home, the uncertainty can lead to anxiety regarding your family's health and the stability of your property. Mold Testing in Burlington, NJ, and Mercer County offers definitive answers through scientific air and surface evaluations, allowing you to comprehend precisely what is occurring within your walls, attic, or basement.

Given Burlington's closeness to the Delaware River and New Jersey's humid seasonal variations, moisture issues frequently create optimal conditions for concealed mold proliferation. Air & Surface Testing accurately measures mold levels in your home with scientific rigor. You will also receive Certified Lab Analysis, ensuring you have precise and trustworthy test outcomes, as well as Moisture & Leak Detection to pinpoint the source of any potential mold issues.

🔬

Certified Laboratory Partnership

Every sample collected by Mold Recovery Plus is dispatched to an AIHA-accredited, third-party microbiology laboratory. This guarantees 100% objective data free from any conflict of interest.

What Is Included in Every Mold Test?

  • ✓
    Outdoor Baseline Air Comparison: We capture an exterior air cassette to establish ambient regional spore counts. Interior cassettes are measured against this baseline to establish whether indoor spore elevation exists.
  • ✓
    Direct Surface Swabs or Bio-Tape Lifts: Applied to suspicious discoloration on baseboards, cinder blocks, floor joists, or HVAC supply vents to differentiate active fungi from soot or cosmetic mineral efflorescence.
  • ✓
    Moisture Gradient Mapping: Electronic pin and pinless moisture meters check subfloors and wall cavities to confirm active dampness behind the test area.
  • ✓
    Clear, Easy-to-Read Remediation Protocol: We translate the laboratory's technical data into plain English. We explain every finding so you never feel pressured or confused by jargon.

When Should You Inquire About Mold Testing?

Testing is not necessary for every situation; if you already have large patches of visible mold, remediation is the obvious immediate priority. However, mold testing is essential in scenarios such as:

  • • Real Estate Transactions: Home buyers or sellers requiring certified clearance before closing.
  • • Unidentified Odors: Chronic musty or damp smells where visual inspection reveals no visible colonies.
  • • Post-Remediation Verification: Ensuring spore counts inside the living envelope match or beat natural outdoor air before taking down containment barriers.
  • • Unexplained Health Sensitivities: When occupants experience unexplained respiratory or allergy symptoms indoors.

Testing FAQs

Standard lab turnaround is 48 to 72 business hours. Rush processing (24-hour turnaround) is available upon request for time-sensitive real estate closings.
