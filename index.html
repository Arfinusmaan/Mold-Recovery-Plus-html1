Answers Property Owners Need to Know

Clear, honest information about mold inspection, testing, costs, and remediation safety.

A mold inspection is a comprehensive physical and thermal evaluation of your property to identify moisture sources, active leaks, and visible mold colonization. Mold testing, on the other hand, collects scientific air and surface swab samples sent to an accredited independent laboratory to identify the specific mold genus and measure airborne spore concentrations.
We use our exclusive Technologically Advanced Vapor System (TAVS). First, active surface growth is treated with a safe surface mold killer that removes staining and applies a protective barrier. Then, the entire room is treated with vapor fogging that penetrates deep into materials and treats the ambient air to ensure airborne spores are thoroughly eliminated.
